CHEME 3130 · Team project

Cornell CHP Plant

Understanding the tradeoffs in energy systems.

Project notes

Aspen modeling of Cornell’s combined heat and power plant, exploring efficiency and operating conditions.

Read the team report ↗

Overview

Our team modeled the plant’s combined Brayton and Rankine cycles in Aspen to explore how inlet conditions influence efficiency and performance.

The approach

We compared modeled operating scenarios and considered fuel use, power output, costs, feasibility, and alternative fuel sourcing.

The optimized scenario increased modeled efficiency while reducing energy output. The report discusses that tradeoff and energy inputs outside the simplified model.
