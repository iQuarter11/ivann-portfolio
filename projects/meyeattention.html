Cornell DEBUT · Team project

MeyeAttention

Turning pupillary responses into signals worth understanding.

Role / Product Manager

Device development · Machine learning · Pupillometry

The project

MeyeAttention is a wearable pupillometry platform exploring neurological screening, including signatures associated with ADHD, traumatic brain injury, and other central nervous system conditions.

My contribution

I’ve led an interdisciplinary engineering team through product definition, device development, computational integration, testing, and validation. My technical work includes CNN and logistic regression models, a pipeline from data acquisition to classification, and pupillometry-derived biomarkers.

From wearable to measurement

The prototype combines a lightweight, 3D-printed headset with an infrared camera and emitter, plus a chest module containing a Raspberry Pi, display, and battery. Cognitive-task timing is synchronized with video capture.

A DeepLabCut neural network identifies four boundary points on each pupil. The analysis pipeline cleans tracking data, removes artifacts such as blinks, and calculates pupil diameter over time to study attention-related responses.

Prototype evaluation

The 2025 competition submission reports a held-out pupil-coordinate tracking RMSE of 2.62 pixels. This measures spatial tracking error, rather than clinical diagnostic accuracy. The prototype is designed to support research toward a brief, accessible assessment of attention.

A team milestone

MeyeAttention won first place overall at the 2025 BMES Medtronic Student Design Competition.
