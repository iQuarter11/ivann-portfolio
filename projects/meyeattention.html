Cornell DEBUT · Team project

MeyeAttention

Turning pupillary responses into signals worth understanding.

Role / Product Manager

Device development · Machine learning · Pupillometry

The project

MeyeAttention is a wearable pupillometry platform exploring neurological screening, including signatures associated with ADHD, traumatic brain injury, and other central nervous system conditions.

My contribution

I’ve led an interdisciplinary engineering team through product definition, device development, computational integration, testing, and validation. My technical work includes CNN and logistic regression models, a pipeline from data acquisition to classification, and pupillometry-derived biomarkers.

A team milestone

MeyeAttention won first place overall at the 2025 BMES Medtronic Student Design Competition.
