BIONB 4910 · Principles of Neurophysiology

Imaging Neural Signals

Using fluorescence to explore the activity beneath the surface.

Course project · Spring 2025

Fluorescence microscopy · ImageJ · Neural signaling

Read the course paper ↗

The question

How can fluorescence microscopy reveal specific neural structures and changes in activity? This course paper examines ion transport and synaptic signaling in transgenic Drosophila larvae.

Imaging and analysis

The work uses GFP-based labeling and calcium-sensitive fluorescence to examine neural preparations under baseline, stimulated, and elevated-potassium conditions. Images captured with a CMOS camera and Pylon software were quantified using ImageJ.

What I explored

The analysis connects fluorescence changes with neural excitability and signaling, then considers the strengths and limitations of optical imaging compared with electrophysiological recording, including photobleaching, temporal resolution, and imaging depth.

An academic course paper. Transgenic preparations were provided by Dr. David Deitcher; dissections were performed by Dr. Bruce Johnson and the BIONB 4910 teaching staff.
