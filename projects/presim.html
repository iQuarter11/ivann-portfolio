PreSim

Engineering education, one process at a time.

Project notes

A chemical engineering learning platform in development, designed to connect process concepts, flowsheets, and material balances before moving into simulation.

GitHub ↗

Overview

PreSim is being developed to help students work from a process idea toward a structured specification, with explicit inputs, assumptions, and engineering constraints.

The approach

The planned experience combines a React interface with language model assistance for process specification. Conservation laws, unit consistency, and benchmark cases are central to the design.

This project is in development. Screenshots and a walkthrough will be added as it takes shape.
